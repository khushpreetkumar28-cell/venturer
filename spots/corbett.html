Jim Corbett & Rajaji

The Frontiers of Apex Predators & Ancient Shivalik Trails

About the Reserves

Spanning the rugged foothills of the Outer Himalayas in Uttarakhand, Jim Corbett National Park and Rajaji National Park form a massive, contiguous wildlife ecosystem. Dominated by dense Sal forests, sprawling grasslands (Chaurs), and gravelly riverbeds carved by the Ramganga and Ganges, this terrain is world-renowned. It serves as a fortress for the endangered Bengal tiger and a primary migration corridor for massive herds of wild Asian elephants.

The Vibe

This wilderness carries a tense, raw, and deeply thrilling tracker energy. Moving through these corridors, the silence of the ancient woodland is constantly broken by the frantic warning calls of langurs and barking deer, signaling a hidden predator nearby. It is a world ruled entirely by natural law—where cold, misty mornings find you navigating deep jungle tracks in open vehicles, scanning the brush for camouflaged stripes and monumental shadows.

Epic Adventures to Try

Dhikala Deep-Zone Safari

Penetrate deep into Corbett's core wilderness zone in a high-clearance open 4x4. Traverse the sweeping grasslands of Dhikala to track the apex Bengal tiger along river channels.

Chilla Elephant Track Expedition

Navigate the rugged, dry riverbeds and steep Shivalik foothills of Rajaji's Chilla sector. Track massive breeding herds of wild Asian elephants moving through open scrub forests.

Ramganga Back-Country Rafting

Paddle down the calmer, wilderness stretches of the Ramganga River flanking the boundary walls of Corbett. Look out for gharials and marsh crocodiles basking along sandy banks.

Buffer Zone Forest Camping

Set up camp in secure adventure lodges located inside the forested buffer zones. Spend cold nights around wood-fire pits listening to the deep, echoing sounds of the sub-Himalayan jungle.

Useful Information

  • Best Time for Action: November to mid-May when the core park gates are fully open and drying waterholes force big game out into open viewpoints.
  • Monsoon Complete Closure: All core zones (like Dhikala and Chilla) are completely closed down from June to October due to heavy rains washing away dirt tracks.
  • Strict Booking Barriers: Night stays inside core forest rest houses must be booked online via official portals up to 45 days in advance. Slots fill up instantly.
  • Jungle Discipline: Stepping out of your vehicle inside safari tracks is strictly forbidden by federal law. Maintain absolute silence to avoid altering wildlife behavior.
