Bir Billing

The Paragliding Capital of India

About Bir Billing

Tucked away in the scenic Joginder Nagar Valley of Himachal Pradesh, Bir Billing is globally renowned as one of the finest aero-sports sites on Earth. The destination is perfectly split into two: Billing, the high-altitude launch pad sitting at 2,400 meters, and Bir, the sprawling Tibetan colony landing zone below. With ideal thermal dynamics and pristine mountain ridges, it plays host to international paragliding world cups.

The Vibe

Bir Billing has a uniquely peaceful yet adventurous pulse. It is a harmonious blend of serene Tibetan Buddhist monastic culture and high-flying adrenaline culture. The skies are constantly painted with dozens of colorful paragliders drifting silently overhead, while the streets below are filled with cozy indie cafes, eco-friendly hostels, and pilots unpacking their wings alongside monks walking down quiet, pine-lined lanes.

Epic Adventures to Try

Tandem & Cross-Country Paragliding

Take a leap off the cliffs of Billing for a 20-to-30-minute flight. Catch thermal updrafts to glide effortlessly over deep forest basins before touching down safely in Bir fields.

Rajgundha Valley Trekking

Hike along old mountain trails connecting Billing to the pristine semi-nomadic village of Rajgundha. Cross wooden bridges and cold mountain streams along the way.

Billing Ridge Camping

Pitch your tent directly at the high take-off site. Watch an unobstructed, spectacular panoramic sunset over the Kangra Valley and sleep right beneath a clear dome of stars.

Useful Information

  • Best Time for Action: September to November for the absolute best flying thermals; March to May for great trekking conditions and lush greenery.
  • Monsoon Shutdown: The paragliding launch site is completely closed down by government authorities from July to mid-September due to safety risks from heavy rains.
  • Eco-Rules: Bir is highly focused on sustainable tourism. Ensure you dispose of waste responsibly and respect the quiet zones around the monasteries.
  • Getting to the Top: Local shared taxis operate continuously from the Bir landing site up to the Billing take-off cliff, taking roughly 45 minutes along a winding mountain road.
