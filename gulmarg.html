Gulmarg

The Ultimate Powdershred Kingdom

About Gulmarg

Situated in the Pir Panjal Range of the Western Himalayas at a towering altitude of 2,650 meters, Gulmarg is legendary. Known across the globe for its massive annual snowfall and deep backcountry bowls, it transitions from a sweeping meadow of wild alpine flowers in summer into Asia's premier high-altitude ski and snowboard paradise come winter.

The Vibe

Gulmarg feels like a raw, extreme frontiersman outpost wrapped in a serene mountain embrace. The atmosphere is heavily charged with winter stoke—global freeriders tuning their edge waxes in cozy wooden cabins, avalanche beacons clicking on at dawn, and lines of eager skiers gazing up at the massive spine of Mt. Apharwat. It is a pure backcountry playground where mountain grit meets pristine Himalayan quiet.

Epic Adventures to Try

Backcountry Skiing & Boarding

Shred Gulmarg's world-famous dry, light powder snow. Drop into massive ungroomed bowls, steep glades, and natural halfpipes off the Apharwat peak.

Apharwat Gondola Expedition

Ride one of the highest cable cars in the world, ascending to a dizzying 3,979 meters. Ascend straight through the clouds to witness untamed boundary ridges.

Alpather F Trerozen Lakek

A challenging high-altitude winter trek to a breathtaking lake that remains completely frozen well into June. Hike across vast, dramatic snowpack basins.

Wild Wilderness Snowmobiling

Blast across open sub-alpine snowfields at high speeds. Cruise through pine-forested valley trails on heavy-duty, high-performance winter sleds.

Useful Information

  • Best Time for Action: January to March for deep winter powder skiing; June to September for high alpine trekking and mountain golf.
  • Atmospheric Pressure: You are sleeping above 2,600m and ascending past 3,900m on the Gondola. Take Phase 1 slow to fully acclimatize.
  • Safety Gear: Avalanche transceivers, shovels, and probes are mandatory if you intend to step past the marked ski boundaries into Phase 2 zones.
  • Booking Tips: Phase 2 Gondola tickets sell out exceptionally fast during peak winter freeride seasons. Secure your boarding slots online well in advance.
