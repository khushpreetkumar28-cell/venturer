Our Next Trip

Vanue:- Rishikesh

Plunge into the roaring capital of churning rapids and massive vertical drops. This upcoming expedition shifts our frontier from the coast straight into the rugged limestone gorges of Rishikesh, Uttarakhand. Prepare to battle cold, glacier-fed currents on intense white-water rafting runs, launch yourself from a massive fixed-cantilever platform hanging 83 meters over a raw wilderness ravine, and swing through giant gravitational arcs facing sheer Himalayan walls. We will transition from high-velocity aerial action to authentic, rugged riverside camping right on the sandbanks of the Ganges. Out here, the noise of the modern world drops away, replaced entirely by the sound of raging waters and pure adrenaline.

🗺️ The Tactical Itinerary (5-Day Basecamp Schedule)

  • Day 01: Dawn Arrival & Basecamp Check-in
    • Morning: Early morning arrival in Rishikesh. Check in to our primary accommodation, Golden Healing Journeys Luxury Homestays, to drop heavy gear and acclimatize.
    • Afternoon: Local foot tracking around the iconic suspension landmarks of Laxman Jhula and Ram Jhula.
    • Evening: Attend the intense, sunset Ganga Aarti ceremony at Triveni Ghat before turning in early.
  • Day 02: Vertical Plunges at Mohan Chatti
    • Morning: Move out via 4x4 to the extreme zone at Mohan Chatti. Gear up, complete medical checks, and execute the Mohan Chatti Bungee Jump—diving 83 meters down into a sheer rock canyon.
    • Afternoon: Strap into a full tactical harness for the Extreme Giant Swing, experiencing massive gravitational acceleration over the valley floor.
    • Evening: Return to basecamp to review video captures and rest.
  • Day 03: Transition to Shivpuri Wilderness
    • Morning: Pack light expedition gear and check out of the homestay. Drive up into the dense, forested hills of Shivpuri.
    • Afternoon: Check-in to your Shivpuri Riverside Camping setup right on the white sand beaches of the Ganges.
    • Evening: Wilderness survival orientation, outdoor beach volleyball, and a night campfire right along the roaring riverbanks.
  • Day 04: Conquering the White-Water Currents
    • Morning: Comprehensive safety briefing and paddle training. Launch our expedition rafts directly into the freezing, churning waters of the Ganges.
    • Afternoon: Complete a grueling White-Water Rafting run, smashing through legendary Grade III and IV rapids like 'The Wall', 'Roller Coaster', and 'Golf Course'.
    • Evening: Return to the riverside beach camp for a final dinner under a completely clear night sky.
  • Day 05: Ridge Tracking & Extraction
    • Morning: Short wilderness foot trek up to hidden mountain waterfalls in the Shivpuri buffer forest.
    • Afternoon: Return to camp, pack gear, and transit back to the Rishikesh transit depot.
    • Evening: Board the return bus back to your original pickup hubs (ISBT 17 / 43 / Mohali), arriving back early the next morning.
